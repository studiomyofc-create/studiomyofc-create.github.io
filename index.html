Pipeline Metricas

Pipeline Metricas is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels AnimalDocs, Duelo Salvaje, Duello Selvaggio and Wildes Duell. It reads the channels' own YouTube Analytics statistics (views, audience retention, traffic sources, countries, impressions and click-through rate) so the owner can compare the performance of their videos.

The tool has read-only access. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything, and it is not offered to the public.
