Privacy Policy — Pipeline Metricas

Last updated: October 20, 2026.

Pipeline Metricas is an internal tool used only by the owner of the channels it connects to. It uses Google OAuth with read-only scopes (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly) to read the statistics of the owner's own YouTube channels.

  • Data accessed: channel and video information and aggregated YouTube Analytics reports of the owner's channels. No data about individual viewers is accessed.
  • Use: the data is stored only on the owner's own computer to compare video performance. It is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
  • Retention and removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the locally stored reports can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Pipeline Metricas' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
